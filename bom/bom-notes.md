# BOM notes

`bom/bom.csv` is the equipment list for the reference micro-factory in RFE-PRC-001, numbered to match the exploded view (`media/exploded.png`) and the floor plan RFE-DWG-001. Every line is priced.

- All prices are indicative USD for concept review, not quotes. They are outside any hardware budget: `budget_usd` in `project.yaml` is null on purpose, because this repo is a playbook, not a single machine. The list is checked against the R10 value-engineering target ($25,000 for items 1 to 12) instead.
- Equipment total for items 1 to 12: **$25,505**. R10 is a value-engineering target of $25,000 (a hypothetical control target, not a limit); the estimate is $505 over it. Item 13 (building shell, about $9,200 for the container option) depends on the site and is listed separately. RFE-CAL-001 section 9 recomputes the total from this file.
- Changes at TRL 3 (2026-09-25): the shredder line gains a lined acoustic enclosure ($600, R12); the fume line gains a second enclosing hood and an impregnated carbon stage ($300, R11); the electrical board gains a heater interlock for the decided single-phase supply ($300, RFE-DDR-001 item 5); the press line names its cooling press; the fire extinguishers go from two to three.
- Changes on 2026-09-25 from the recommendations accepted by Amish (RFE-DDR-002): the fume fan goes from 1.1 kW to 1.5 kW (+$150, total $24,050 to $24,200); the electrical board note gives the new maximum demand of about 8.8 kW (38 A); the building shell line puts a rented shed first and allows the container option only with a structural engineer's design.
- Changes on 2026-09-30 for construction (RFE-DDR-003, made under Amish's 2026-09-30 instruction to make the design physically buildable; open for his review): drying fan stand (+$50, item 4); transfer bridge between the presses (+$250, item 6); sliding sash with two booth openings, booth take-off, two duct wall brackets, wall sleeve and outside fan stand (+$400, item 7); plywood backboard and cable tray (+$400, item 11); seam beam, floor seam plate and roof flashing for the container option (+$1,200, item 13). Items 3 and 12 changed in specification only (lower enclosure roof with the chute at 1.70 m and the door on the aisle face; cage gate on the aisle face). Total $24,200 to $25,300.
- Changes on 2026-10-02 to carry Amish's decisions of 2026-10-02 into the design: cranked feed hood on the shredder chute collar so the ISO 13857 reach check passes, and three self-closing spring hinges on the enclosure door (+$105, item 3); foam padding and hazard tape on the cable tray crossing over the aisle (+$40, item 11); two pairs of self-closing gate hinges on the export cage (+$60, item 12). Prices are hardware retail and local workshop rates, indicative. Total $25,300 to $25,505. No mass is listed for this layout; the added parts are light (under 15 kg together) and change no floor or wall loading.
- Suppliers are supplier types (local fabricator, open-hardware workspace, HVAC supplier, licensed electrician). No purchasing list or quotes are part of TRL 3.
- Excluded: land, rent, permits, grid connection, working capital, transport, and operating costs (wages, energy, PPE replacement, filters, wear parts). Operating costs are in the economics model (`docs/playbook/economics_model.py`).
- Machines marked "make" are open designs built by a local fabricator or an open-hardware workspace. The published Precious Plastic Shredder Pro, Extrusion Pro and sheetpress are specified for 400 V; this list assumes single-phase variants (drives with single-phase input for the motors, a derated 5 kW press), which RFE-CAL-001 flags as a risk to throughput (R6).
