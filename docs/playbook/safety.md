# Safety and environmental rules

- No open burning of any material.
- No acid or cyanide leaching of electronics or batteries.
- Fume extraction for all plastic melting; PVC is never heated.
- Furnace work requires eye, face and foot protection and a dry sand floor.
- Lithium cells are stored in fireproof containers and never punctured.
- Lithium cells are pulled out first at intake, terminals taped, and kept out of the shredder.
- Medical, chemical and hazardous waste is refused at the door; sharps go in a sharps container.
- The shredder is guarded, its hopper is interlocked, and its area is a hearing protection zone.
- Every circuit near water has a 30 mA RCD; wash water and sludge go to a licensed disposal route.
- No workers under 18.

The reference micro-factory applies these rules in the safety section of RFE-PRC-001 (`docs/02-concept.md`).
