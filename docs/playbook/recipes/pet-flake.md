# Recipe: PET clean flake

**Output:** clean, dry, color-sorted PET flake of about 5 to 10 mm, sold by the bag as feedstock to a regional recycler. PET is not melted in the reference micro-factory: it needs drying to about 0.005 % moisture before melt processing, which a small site cannot do reliably, and it degrades easily in small extruders (RFE-DDR-001, item 3).

## Input grade

- Clear and light-blue PET bottles and trays (resin code 1), emptied. Green and brown bottles in a separate lot.
- Caps (usually PP or HDPE) and rings removed at the sorting table where possible; the float-sink step removes the rest.
- Rejected at intake: bottles that held oil, fuel, pesticides or other chemicals; PVC bottles (resin code 3; they look similar and sink with PET, so they must be caught by eye or by the NIR scanner before washing).

## Steps

1. **Sort** clear from colored bottles. Use WasteWise Scan (NIR) on any bottle that might be PVC or PETG.
2. **Pre-wash** whole bottles in the first tank with warm water and detergent; strip labels by hand.
3. **Shred** to 5 to 10 mm flake. Clean the shredder of any previous polymer first.
4. **Float-sink** the flake in the second tank of clean water. PET (1.33 to 1.45 g/cm³) sinks; PP (0.90 to 0.91) and HDPE (0.94 to 0.97) cap flakes float and are skimmed off into their own bins. PVC (1.38 to 1.41) also sinks, which is why step 1 matters.
5. **Rinse** the sunk PET flake, drain and spread on mesh trays at 10 kg/m² or less.
6. **Dry** with the fan until the flake is free-flowing and dry to the touch (surface moisture only; deep drying is the buyer's job). Check with the moisture meter.
7. **Bag** in 25 kg sacks or less, label, weigh and issue the passport.

## Temperature window

No heating. Wash water warm (about 40 to 60 °C) if a heat source is available; cold water works with longer soaking.

## Yield (RFE-CAL-001, estimate)

22 kg of PET in 100 kg of mixed input gives about 19.3 kg of clean flake (about 88 %): 10 % lost to labels, glue, dirt and float-sink rejects, 1.5 % shredder fines and 1 % drying spills.

## Rejects

Labels and glue sludge (licensed disposal); floating cap flakes (to the HDPE and PP lines); PVC found at sorting (residue, never heated); colored flake (separate lot).

## PPE

Puncture-resistant gloves at sorting; waterproof gloves and eye protection at washing; hearing protection in the shredder zone; safety boots throughout.
