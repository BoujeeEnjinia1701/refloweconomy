# Material feasibility matrix

Which materials a small, local operation can recover safely, and where industrial refining takes over. Working draft; values are indicative and will be sourced in TRL 3 work.

| Material | Small-scale feasibility | Route | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plastics (PET, HDPE, PP) | High | Sort, wash, shred, then extrude, injection mold or sheet press | Open machine designs exist; the main issues are sorting quality and fumes |
| Aluminum | High | Sort, remelt at about 660 °C, sand cast | Remelting needs a small fraction of the energy of primary aluminum |
| Steel | Medium | Induction or arc melting; re-rolling | Heavier equipment; rebar re-rolling is already a common small industry |
| Glass | Medium | Crush to sand or aggregate; remelting is energy-hungry | Crushing is simple and safe |
| Lithium cells | Reuse high; recycling low | Test, grade and rebuild into packs | Metal recovery needs industrial chemical processing |
| E-waste metals | Dismantle only | Safe dismantling and sorting; export boards for refining | Informal chemical recovery causes severe harm |
| Ore refining | Out of scope | Not recommended at small scale | Hazardous and capital-intensive |

Principle: recover and remanufacture locally; export only what needs industrial refining.
