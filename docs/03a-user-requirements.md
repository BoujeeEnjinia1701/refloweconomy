# ReflowEconomy user requirements

What the people who would run, work in, sell to and regulate a micro-factory need, written from their side. Product requirements that meet these needs are in RFE-PRS-001 (`03-requirements.md`) and the links are in the traceability matrix RFE-RTM-001 (`03b-traceability.md`). Method: STANDARDS section 22. These are first-pass needs taken from the problem statement and its sources; none has yet been confirmed in a co-design session with the first partner (a waste-picker cooperative; first candidate SWaCH, Pune, nothing agreed), and all are to be revised from what that partner says.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Performance | What to process here | We need to know which materials we can recover safely ourselves and which must go to a licensed refiner | High | Micro-factory operator; municipality (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-002 | Usability | Recipes we can follow | We need step-by-step recipes for each material we process: what goes in, temperatures, what comes out, what protection to wear | High | Micro-factory operator; line worker (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-003 | Cost | Know if it pays | Before we spend money, we need to see whether a site can pay its way at our own prices, wages and tariff | High | Micro-factory operator (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-004 | Logistics | Send away safely | We need to know how to pack, label and where to send what we cannot process | Medium | Industrial refiner or exporter (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-005 | Deployment and assembly | Fit a small site | It has to fit in a rented shed or two shipping containers of about 60 m², with room to move safely | High | Micro-factory operator (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-006 | Performance | Handle a day's collection | It must handle what a cooperative brings in a day, about 100 kg a shift | High | Micro-factory operator; waste picker (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-007 | Performance | Keep value local | Most of the value of what we collect should stay with us and our community, not leave as cheap scrap | High | Waste picker or cooperative member; municipality (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-008 | Environment and durability | Little to disposal, nothing burned | As little as possible should go to disposal, and nothing is ever burned | High | Municipality; waste picker (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-009 | Cost | Low electricity bill | The electricity bill must not eat the margin | Medium | Micro-factory operator (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-010 | Deployment and assembly | Use the supply we can get | It must run on the electricity connection a small site can actually get | High | Micro-factory operator; electricity distribution company (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-011 | Safety | Clean air | We must be able to work a full shift without breathing harmful fumes | High | Line worker (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-012 | Safety | Safe machines | We must be able to work near the shredder, hot presses, noise and electricity without being hurt | High | Line worker (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-013 | Regulatory | Trusted material | Buyers need to trust what they buy: polymer, grade, contamination and where it came from | High | Local buyer of recycled material (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-014 | Usability | Quick records | Recording a lot must not slow the work | Medium | Micro-factory operator (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-015 | Maintenance | Build and repair locally | The machines and the fit-out must be things a local fabricator can make and repair | Medium | Micro-factory operator; open hardware community (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-016 | Cost | Affordable equipment | The equipment must be within reach of a cooperative's capital | Medium | Micro-factory operator (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-017 | Regulatory | Permittable | The authorities must be able to register and permit the site | High | Municipality; state pollution control board (PRB Table 2) |
| UR-018 | Safety | Fire safety | We must be protected from fires, including fires started by lithium cells hidden in the input | High | Line worker; municipality (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-019 | Usability | Fair intake rules | Rules for refusing or charging for poor loads must be clear and fair to pickers | Medium | Waste picker or cooperative member (PRB Table 1) |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
